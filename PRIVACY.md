# Privacy Policy

WeatherMaster is an open-source application.

We respect your privacy. The application itself does **not**:

- Collect, store, or share personal information.
- Track your IP address, usage data, or device identifiers.
- Send personal data to us directly.

---

## Third-Party Services & APIs

WeatherMaster relies on external weather and geolocation services to provide forecasts and
location-based functionality.

These third-party services may process certain technical information, including:

- IP address
- Approximate or precise location data
- Device or network-related information required for API requests

This data processing is handled by the respective service providers under their own privacy
policies. WeatherMaster itself does not control or store this data.

---

## Location Permission

The app may request access to your device's location to display local weather information.

- Granting location permission is optional.
- Your precise location is used locally within the app to fetch weather data from external APIs.
- WeatherMaster does not store your precise location.

---

## Children

This application is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information.
